04How the IHBAS paper feels

Mostly direct one-liners.Who developed which test or therapy, definitions, criteria and durations, brain structures, statistics basics.
Easy-to-moderate overall— but with a handful of “outlier” questions on old psychiatric terms, rare syndromes and lesser-known concepts.
A few recurring Indian topics.Indian tests (ISAA, VSMS, MISIC), MHCA 2017, RCI and RPwD — usually one or two simple questions.
Very little Match, Assertion–Reason or long cases.So speed and accurate recall matter more than long reasoning.

05Your 90-minute game plan

  1. First pass, 55 minutes: answer everything you know at once; mark anything doubtful for review.
  2. Second pass, 25 minutes: return to marked questions and eliminate options.
  3. Last 10 minutes: fill every blank. There is no negative marking, so a guess can only help.
